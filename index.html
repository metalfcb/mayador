Questions parents ask

A little clarity before the magic.

Is Mayador educational or entertaining?

Both. Each story has a deliberate habit, value or thinking skill at its heart, but the lesson lives inside a genuinely fun adventure. The child experiences it without feeling taught.

Why make my child the main character?

Personal relevance creates attention and emotion. When children see themselves facing the situation and making the choice, the experience becomes more vivid, memorable and easier to discuss later.

What age group are Mayador stories for?

Mayador is being designed for children aged 3–12, with themes, language and challenges adapted to different age bands.
